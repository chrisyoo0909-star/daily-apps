Shepherd

Five fields. One dog. A lot of very anxious sheep.
Move the dog with your mouse, or drag on touch. Arrow keys / WASD work too.
Sheep flee from the dog and stick together — push the flock from behind, don't charge into it.
Goal: get every sheep through the gate into the pen. Beat the par time for three stars.

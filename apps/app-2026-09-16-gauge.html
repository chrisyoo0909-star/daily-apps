Resistances are NEC Chapter 9 Table 8 (uncoated conductors at 75 °C); ampacities are NEC Table 310.16 at 30 °C ambient, derated from the 90 °C column and then capped at your termination rating. 14/12/10 AWG are also held to the 15/20/30 A small-conductor limit. AC figures ignore conductor reactance, which matters on large conductors in steel conduit.

Skyshift

A day-by-day light and sleep plan that shifts your body clock before you fly — so you land closer to local time.

Bedtime → wake time
3 days
Built on standard circadian phase-shifting practice: your body clock's low point (core-temperature minimum) sits about 2 h before your normal wake time,
light after it pulls you earlier, light before it pushes you later. Educational tool — not medical advice.

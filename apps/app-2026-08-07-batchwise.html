Conversions use standard values (1 cup = 236.6 ml, 1 oz = 28.35 g, 1 stick butter = 113 g), then round to measurable amounts; “≈” marks a quantity nudged to the nearest eighth. Teaspoons and tablespoons stay put — measuring spoons are the same worldwide. Oven temperatures and inch pan sizes convert, but times and temperatures are never scaled.
